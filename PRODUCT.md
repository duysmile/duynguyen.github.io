# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
Astro static site, deployed on Netlify at duysmile.com (user chose Astro over keeping the 2019 CRA app).

## Users
- **Hiring managers abroad** (remote / international roles). They skim the home page in under a minute, then open the CV.
- **Fellow engineers** who read the blog and side projects.

## Product Purpose
Personal site of Nguyen Duy ("Bean"), full-stack engineer, 7+ years. It makes years of unreflected work visible: who he is, what he built, what he writes. Success: a hiring manager understands his level fast and reaches the CV; an engineer finds work worth reading.

## Positioning
**He owns it end to end.** He takes products from blank page to production and keeps them running: Xendit Offline Payments (1+ year live with zero critical payment incidents), Pokerage backend, VNLP chatbot platform — all started from scratch.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Pages: home, CV (printable, ≤ 2 A4), projects, blog (from `github.com/duysmile/blogs-github/blogs` at build time), 404.
- English only. Employers named; end clients described generically.
- No phone number, no address, no private reviews or interview feedback.

## Brand Commitments
- Nickname **Bean**; line "bean @ thinker, life lover".
- Voice: warm, personal, first person, plain English.
- Photo and football: not to be used.
- Character (user, 2026-10-29): calm, humble, conservative. A backend engineer at his computer. No loud colors, gimmicks, or self-hype.

## Evidence on Hand
- Facts: `docs/changes/2026-10-29-portfolio-rebuild/profile.md` (merged CV 2026 + CV 2023 + Xendit notes). Do not add numbers or claims beyond it.
- Award: Best Employee of the Year, EM&AI 2020.
- Side projects: public GitHub repos under `duysmile`.
- No testimonials, logos, or press exist. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
1. Show the work, not adjectives: every claim ties to a shipped thing.
2. Fast to scan for a hiring manager; deep for an engineer who stays.
3. Personal but professional: Bean's voice, no gimmicks.
4. One data file for facts; content stays easy to update.
